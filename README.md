# InMotion

### Your movement is the controller.

InMotion is a browser-based workout arcade that turns running in place, jumping, squatting and holding still into actions inside a 3D game. Your computer displays the world, and your phone captures your movement from your pocket. No headset or dedicated motion controller is needed.

Built for **canivibecodeit presents THE BUILD GAMES**.

## Four ways to get moving

| Game | How you play | What you practise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Endless Runner** | Run in place through a subway-inspired world. Jump over obstacles, squat to slide beneath barriers, collect coins and progress through increasingly demanding levels. The game handles lane selection. | Sustained movement, reaction timing and coordination. |
| **Red Light, Green Light** | Move toward the finish during green light, then freeze when the doll turns. Jump over fallen obstacles and squat to collect helpful items. Reach the line before time runs out. | Stop-and-go movement, balance and body control. |
| **Jump Rope** | Run in place to cross the bridge. Time your jumps as the giant rope sweeps beneath you, and clear the gap along the way. | Rhythm, jump timing and coordination. |
| **Track & Field** | Race around a stadium track against computer-controlled runners. Keep your pace, jump the hurdles and complete your chosen number of laps. | Pacing, endurance and hurdle timing. |

All four games are silent. Watch the scene, movement prompts and on-screen indicators for instructions.

## Why play?

InMotion gives everyday movement a clear purpose: reach the next checkpoint, clear an obstacle or beat your previous result. Short rounds make it easy to take an active break, while different challenges give you reasons to practise more than one movement.

- **Make movement engaging.** React to a game instead of watching a timer.
- **Use the devices you already have.** A phone and a computer are enough to get started.
- **Mix your movements.** Alternate running, jumping, squatting and controlled pauses.
- **Follow your progress.** Review saved sessions and activity estimates in your personal stats.
- **Play at your own pace.** Start with a comfortable effort and build up as you learn the games.

Calories and distance are gameplay estimates, not medical measurements. Fitness results depend on your effort, consistency and individual circumstances.

## What you need

- A computer with a current browser and working 3D graphics support.
- A smartphone with motion sensors, using Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android.
- Both devices connected to the same Wi-Fi network, or a computer connected by Ethernet to the same router.
- A secure front pocket and enough clear space to move comfortably.

You can also display the computer on a TV using HDMI. Playing directly in a TV browser is a beta feature, and compatibility varies.

## How to play

1. **Open InMotion on your computer.** Let the carousel finish its entrance animation. The phone connection panel will then appear.
2. **Connect your phone.** Scan the QR with your phone camera, or open the phone link shown on screen and enter the six-digit code. The panel closes once the devices connect, so you can browse the games immediately.
3. **Enable motion.** On your phone, tap **Enable motion** and allow the requested permission. Allow Local Network access if your browser or phone asks for it.
4. **Choose a game.** Browse the carousel on your computer or phone. Your first session guides you through movement training so the game can learn your controller movements.
5. **Position your phone.** Put it securely in your right front pocket, with the screen facing your leg and the top pointing down. A snug pocket helps the game read your movement consistently.
6. **Follow the warm-up.** Stand still when prompted, then use the phone's buttons or the requested movement to start. Watch the screen for when to run, jump, squat or freeze.
7. **Review your session.** Check your personal stats, replay a round or return to the carousel to try another game.

Keep your phone screen unlocked and the controller page open while playing. Pocket mode helps prevent accidental touches; hold two fingers on the screen for one second to unlock that mode.

## Your phone, your controller

The phone shows the current game's instructions and available actions. You can use it to choose a game, continue through prompts and return to the carousel.

Motion travels directly between your phone and computer over your shared network. Internet access is used to open the site and set up the connection. A restricted guest network can prevent devices from connecting; use a network that allows them to communicate with each other.

If motion stops, active gameplay pauses. Return to the phone page and enable motion again if prompted. If the connection drops, follow the reconnect instructions. Stopping motion alone does not lock you out of the carousel.

## Personal progress

Your profile, movement calibration and recorded activity are saved in your browser. Session history helps you look back at your play and compare your own results. Keep using the same browser and site address to retain those records; clearing browser data can remove them.

The current beta focuses on personal progress. A shared online leaderboard is not available yet.

## Play comfortably

Choose a clear space with a stable surface, secure your phone and start gently. Take breaks when you need them and stop if a movement feels uncomfortable. You never need to jump onto furniture or move toward the computer: the running happens in place.
